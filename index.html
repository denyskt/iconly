Iconly: Icon Maker

A focused, native macOS app for designing cross-platform app icons. Compose SF Symbols, shapes, and text on a 1024 × 1024 canvas; export Xcode AppIcon.appiconset or Android mipmap-* launcher sets.

Privacy Policy

Iconly respects your privacy. Your designs, projects, and preferences are stored locally on your Mac. We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or contact details, and we have no user accounts or servers of our own.

We use third-party analytics services to understand how the app is used and to diagnose crashes, so we can improve functionality and stability.

This app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which may collect anonymous data such as:

  • App interactions (e.g. screens viewed, actions taken)
  • Device information (e.g. operating system version, device model)
  • Approximate location (country or region)
  • Crash diagnostics (stack traces, app state at crash time)

This data is anonymous, not linked to your identity, and is used solely for analytics and app improvement. It is not used for advertising or for tracking you across other apps and websites.

Iconly uses Apple's StoreKit framework to handle the optional one-time Pro unlock purchase. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction. Analytics data may be processed by Google Firebase in accordance with their privacy policy.

For questions about this policy, contact denys.kotenko@icloud.com.
