Iconly: Icon Maker

A focused, native macOS app for designing cross-platform app icons. Compose SF Symbols, shapes, and text on a 1024 × 1024 canvas; export Xcode AppIcon.appiconset or Android mipmap-* launcher sets.

Privacy Policy

Iconly respects your privacy. It does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

All your designs and preferences are stored locally on your Mac. There are no user accounts, no telemetry, no third-party analytics, and no advertising SDKs.

Iconly uses Apple's StoreKit framework to handle the optional one-time Pro unlock purchase. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction. Purchase confirmation flows entirely between you and Apple — no purchase data is shared with the developer beyond what Apple reports through App Store Connect (aggregate sales counts, never tied to your identity).

For questions about this policy, contact denys.kotenko@icloud.com.
