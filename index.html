Live canvas

Pinch-zoom, the system squircle mask, and every change rendered as you make it — the preview matches what actool will produce.

🧱

Compose in layers

Stack rectangles, circles, capsules, and text. Reorder, lock, hide, and tune each layer's opacity, scale, rotation, alignment, padding, and optical offset.

🖼️

Bring your own artwork

Import PNG or SVG as a layer. SVG stays crisp at every export size, and an optional recolor tints monochrome glyphs to match your palette.

🌈

Gradients and solid fills

A live two-stop gradient editor with angle control, solid fills, and strokes — on the background and on any layer.

📦

Export for both platforms

An AppIcon.appiconset for iOS and macOS Xcode projects (single-1024 mode for Xcode 14+), or a complete Android mipmap-* launcher set. One design, every size.
